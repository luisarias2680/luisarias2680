# Luis Stephano Arias

**Bioinformatics · Biomedical AI · Scientific Computing**

Systems Engineering undergraduate based in Lima, Peru, working at the intersection of computational biology, biomedical imaging, and machine learning.

My experience includes remote bioinformatics projects connected to ETH Zurich and the University of Bern, spanning large-scale bacterial genome annotation, high-performance computing, single-cell RNA sequencing analysis, and immune-cell annotation.
